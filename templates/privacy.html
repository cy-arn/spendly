Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address and a password (stored in hashed form). As you use Spendly, we also store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We do not collect payment card or bank account details.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Spendly: to sign you in, to save and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you ask for. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the application's database and protected with reasonable technical safeguards. We keep it for as long as your account is active. You can edit or delete your expenses at any time, and you may ask us to delete your account and associated data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may send requests to Google's servers when you visit the site. We do not share your expense data with any third party. If we add other services in the future, we will update this policy to describe them.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request deletion of your data, please contact the Spendly team at support@spendly.example.
